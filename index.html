KILLA CLANKERS

Physics-driven sword combat. Your blade goes exactly where your mouse goes.

  • Mouse — moves the blade. Centered = point forward. Push left/right = raised guard on that side. Up = raise/cock behind shoulder. Down = point at ground.
  • Lock-on — automatic onto the nearest robot in range.
  • A/D — turn your body, or orbit around your locked target.
  • A clean, committed slice across the Neck kills outright, A fast edge through a softened torso cuts a robot clean in two. Bodies ragdoll: knock the wreckage around all you like.
  • Bolted armor plates are permanent — they can't be shattered or cut through. Wherever a plate sits, that spot is protected; hit somewhere unarmored instead.
  • Power-ups drop from every robot you kill — plates, repair cells, heavy arms, servos and new weapons — walk over them to bolt on protection or patch up.
  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · Q/R lean · C crouch · F kick · Esc pause
